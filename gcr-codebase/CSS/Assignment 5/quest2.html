About Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station located in Himachal Pradesh, India. It is surrounded by mountains, forests and rivers. The peaceful environment and scenic views make it a popular destination for travelers.

Popular Places to Visit

  • Solang Valley
  • Rohtang Pass
  • Hadimba Temple
  • Old Manali

Things to Do

Adventure Activities

Enjoy activities such as skiing, paragliding, river rafting and trekking.

Nature & Relaxation

Explore beautiful forests, enjoy mountain views and spend peaceful time near the Beas River.
